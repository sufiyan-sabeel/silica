# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Silica to be a good experience for everyone, regardless
of experience level, background, identity, or how long they have been
programming.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people.
* Assume good faith and ask questions before concluding someone was careless.
* Welcome beginners: explain the reasoning, not just the correction.
* Accept that maintainers may decline a change to keep the language small.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
* Sexualised language or attention, in any project space.
* Publishing others' private information without permission.
* Deliberately derailing technical discussions or repeatedly ignoring maintainer
  decisions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review and any
project communication channel.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the project maintainer (see `LICENSE` for the copyright
holder). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or
block participation, and will explain the reason to the people involved.
